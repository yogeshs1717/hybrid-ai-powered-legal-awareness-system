# Wage Law Research

## Findings
The **Code on Wages, 2019** was implemented and came into effect on **21 November 2025**. Therefore, it supersedes the Payment of Wages Act 1936 and the Minimum Wages Act 1948. 

- **delayed_wages**: Addressed by Code on Wages, 2019, Section 17 (Time limit for payment of wages).
- **unpaid_wages**: Addressed by Code on Wages, 2019, Section 17 (Time limit for payment of wages - specifically subsection 2 regarding termination) and Section 45 (Claims).
- **unauthorized_deduction**: Addressed by Code on Wages, 2019, Section 18 (Deductions which may be made from wages).
- **minimum_wage_violation**: Addressed by Code on Wages, 2019, Section 6 (Fixation of minimum wages).

## Status Updates
The 4 wage issues (`delayed_wages`, `unpaid_wages`, `unauthorized_deduction`, `minimum_wage_violation`) transition from `provision_research_required` to `taxonomy_supported` based on these findings.
