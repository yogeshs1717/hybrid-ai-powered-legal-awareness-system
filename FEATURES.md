# Features & Project Status

**LegalLens** is an AI-powered legal awareness website for Indian citizens. You describe your problem in simple words, and it tells you which Indian laws apply, what steps you should take, and which official government portals you can complain on.

---

## 1. What is Already Included & Working

### 🔍 User Experience & Website
- **Simple Problem Input:** Type your situation in plain English (or pick from ready-made real-life examples).
- **Instant Legal Breakdown:**
  - **Category Identified:** Tells you the legal domain (e.g., Cyber Fraud, Consumer Issue).
  - **Exact Problem Detected:** Identifies the specific situation (e.g., OTP Scam, Defective Product, Refusal of Refund).
  - **Applicable Indian Laws:** Shows the exact legal Act and Section (e.g., IT Act 2000, Consumer Protection Act 2019) with a link to official government text on India Code.
  - **Plain-English Explanations:** Explains in simple words *why* this law applies to your case.
  - **Step-by-Step Next Actions:** Clear checklist of what to do first (e.g., block your card, take screenshots, send a written complaint).
  - **Official Portals:** Direct links to the right government filing websites (like Cybercrime portal, National Consumer Helpline, e-Daakhil, Parivahan).
- **Honest AI (No Hallucinations):**
  - If the system is not confident or no verified law exists for that problem, it honestly tells you instead of making up fake legal advice.
  - Displays a clear legal disclaimer on every result (legal awareness, not a substitute for a lawyer).
- **Clean Modern Look:** Easy-to-use dark mode interface that works smoothly on phones, tablets, and laptops.

### 🌐 Multilingual & Translation Options (English, Hindi, Kannada)
- **Instant Language Switcher:** Dropdown in the navigation bar to switch between **English**, **हिन्दी (Hindi)**, and **ಕನ್ನಡ (Kannada)** at any time.
- **Full Interface Localization:**
  - Navigation, hero banners, example problem chips, buttons, and legal disclaimers all adapt to the selected language.
  - Native language examples ready to click and test.
- **Type in Hindi or Kannada:**
  - Citizens can type their problem directly in Hindi (Devanagari) or Kannada script.
  - The system automatically detects the language and bridges it to the legal analysis engine.
- **Dynamic Result Translation:**
  - One-click language switching right on the analysis results page.
  - Translates the detected legal problem, reasoning, applicable statutory provisions, step-by-step guidance, and official portals into Hindi or Kannada.

### ⚖️ Legal Topics Supported Right Now
The system currently recognizes **14 common legal situations** across 4 main areas:

1. **Cyber Fraud:**
   - OTP scams & banking fraud
   - Phishing & fake links
   - Online impersonation & fake profiles
   - Identity theft
   - Unauthorized account access
2. **Consumer Issues:**
   - Defective products & warranty refusal
   - Refusal of refund or replacement
   - Misleading advertisements
   - Deficiency in paid services
   - Unfair trade practices
3. **Traffic Enforcement:**
   - Police seizing or impounding your vehicle
   - Confiscation of driving license or vehicle papers
   - Showing digital documents (DigiLocker / mParivahan)
4. **Contractual Disputes:**
   - Breach of contract / broken agreements (freelancer non-payment, vendor issues)
